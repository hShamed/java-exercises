# Sixth Exercise

## Instructions

Write a Java program that uses `for` loops to print the following pattern on the console:

```
        *        
      * * *      
    * * * * *    
  * * * * * * *
* * * * * * * * *
      * * *      
      * * *      
```

### Requirements

- Use `for` loops to print the pattern.
- Ensure the output matches the example exactly, including spaces and new lines.
- Use `System.out.print` and `System.out.println` appropriately to control the output format.

### Example Output

```
        *        
      * * *      
    * * * * *    
  * * * * * * *
* * * * * * * * *
      * * *      
      * * *      
```

Good luck!
